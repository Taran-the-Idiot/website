# The ups and downs of organising a global 48 hour hackathon in Sydney

![alt text](https://user-cdn.hackclub-assets.com/01a01705-b2a6-7548-a24d-b72702273c2d/1000039642.jpg)

Last month I ran [Horizons Crux](https://crux.hackclub.com), a 48 hour hackathon in Sydney for high school kids from across the world. Out of all the hackathons I have ever run this was probably one of the hardest things I have ever done. Anyways this whole post is gonna be like a post-mortem type thing of what went well, what went to shit and what could have been better.

Shit to talk about:

- Team communication loops
- Using multiple platforms - keep everything in one place
- Budgets are your best friend
- Prepare your stuff ahead of time
- Get on top of reviews early
- Engagement>signups
- Don't take ease in something "guarunteed"
